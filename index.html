About us

A neighbourhood hardware shop that keeps the whole list in stock.

Most jobs stall because one fitting is missing. We stock deep across plumbing, paints, fasteners and tools, so masons, plumbers, painters and homeowners can finish the list in a single trip — and ask us what actually works before they buy.

The main shop sits in Bohra Colony, Binaki Mangalwari, near Jeeta Bhatti Square. Our branch, Raj Hardware, serves the Mahal side of the city from Aychit Mandir Road.
